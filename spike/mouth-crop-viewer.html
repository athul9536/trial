Crop-first mouth chart

Face landmarks locate the lips, then only that crop is sent for editing. The model never sees the sky or the hands, so it cannot drift them — which is what defeated detecting the mouth after a whole-image edit. Editing a small crop is also faster, cheaper, and spends all the model's resolution on the part that matters.

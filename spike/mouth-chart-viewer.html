Mouth chart — in-style mouth shapes from FLUX.1 Kontext

The mouth rectangle is found automatically: the pixels that changed between the closed and open edits are the mouth, so the crop region is derived from the edit itself rather than guessed by a model or dragged by hand. The sliders are for correcting it, not for setting it.

1 · What the model returned

Look for changes outside the mouth — eyes, hands, background, colour. That is drift, and the crop is what discards it.

detecting…

Orange is every pixel that changed. Numbered boxes are the candidate regions, 1 being the pick. If the mouth is a candidate but not number 1, the score needs work. If it is not there at all, the edit drifted too much.

4 · Motion

Real speech runs about 5 syllables a second. Judge the composite while it is moving at that rate — a seam you can only find by staring may vanish in motion, and a wobble invisible in a still frame becomes obvious once it moves.
